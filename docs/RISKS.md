# Security, privacy and regulatory risks

- Financial harm: never collect credentials, PINs, tokens, authentication codes or financial screenshots; never automate bank operations or use remote control for banking. Customer personally reviews and authorizes through official channels. Future delegated payments require separate legal, banking and regulatory review.
- Identity and physical safety: verified independent providers, approval/suspension workflow, revocable assignment access, approximate offer locations, arrival code, incident escalation and operational support are launch requirements. Demo verification badges describe fictional profiles only.
- LGPD: determine controller/processor roles and legal bases, minimize address/location/health information, define retention, access/export/deletion and breach response. Family relationship alone never grants access. Obtain and record scoped explicit revocable authorization.
- Authorization: server and RLS enforcement for every role, least-privilege RPCs, protected admin role source and rate-limited mutations. Never ship service-role secrets to clients.
- Concurrency: database constraints and transaction locks must protect assignment and reservations. Queue reliability, catch-up processing and real concurrent database tests are prerequisites.
- Accessibility: manual older-adult usability testing and assistive-technology validation remain required. Do not claim WCAG certification from component appearance.
- Marketplace/regulation: obtain Brazilian legal review of consumer terms, independent-worker relationship, cancellation/dispute policy, identity processing, tax and liability obligations. Do not represent Amparo as a financial institution.
- Payments: simulation only. No card collection, transfers, payouts or external payment links. Evaluate provider and compliance before integration.
- Operational readiness: define incident owner, support hours, response times, backups, retention, monitoring, alerting and restore drills before real customers.
